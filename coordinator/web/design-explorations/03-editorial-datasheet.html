Concepts

The coordinator's vocabulary, keyed to its color system.

Exporter
A lab host publishing hardware to the coordinator.
Group
A named cluster of related exporters and places.
Resource
A single controllable device — power, serial, JTAG.
Place (free)
A bundle of hardware you reserve, available now.
Acquire
An exclusive lock on a place while you test.
Reservation
A queued claim, allocated when a place frees.
